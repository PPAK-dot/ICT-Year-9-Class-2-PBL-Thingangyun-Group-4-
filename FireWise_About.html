02 / OUR PURPOSE

Why We Created FireWise

Our objectives guide the development of FireWise and its role in promoting fire safety awareness.

Making Fire Safety Information More Accessible

Fires can affect homes, shops, markets, schools, and workplaces throughout Myanmar. Understanding common fire causes and recognizing possible hazards are important steps towards improving fire safety.

FireWise is an educational website developed to provide accessible fire prevention guidelines, emergency safety information, and digital examples that help users understand fire risks and suitable preventive actions.

Through this project, we also aim to evaluate whether our website helps users improve their understanding of fire safety.

01

Identify Fire Causes

Identify common causes of fires in Myanmar and help users understand potential hazards.

02

Provide Safety Guidelines

Share clear fire prevention guidelines and emergency safety information.

03

Display Digital Fire Alerts

Present digital fire alerts and example risk levels using reliable or clearly labelled simulated data.

04

Recognize and Prevent Hazards

Help users recognize possible fire hazards and understand appropriate preventive actions.

05

Evaluate Learning Outcomes

Evaluate whether the website improves users' fire safety knowledge.
