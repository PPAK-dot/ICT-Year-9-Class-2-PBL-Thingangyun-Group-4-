Learn More About Fire Safety

Practical information for safer homes and communities.

Fire Alarm Placement Tips

Install smoke or heat detectors. Place smoke detectors on every level of your residence. Place them on the ceiling or high on the wall (4 to 12 inches from the ceiling), at the top of open stairways, or at the bottom of enclosed stairs and near (but not in) the kitchen. Test and clean smoke detectors once a month and replace batteries at least once a year. Replace smoke alarms once every 10 years.

Fire Escape System

Review escape routes with your family. Practise escaping from each room. Ensure you have suitable escape ladders if your residence has more than one level. Never block escape routes. Teach family members to stay low to the floor when escaping from a fire and to move outside to a safe meeting point.

Storage Safety Tips

Clean out storage areas. Do not let trash, such as old newspapers and magazines, accumulate. Work areas, aisles, walkways, stairways and equipment should be kept clear of loose materials, trash and scraps. Never block aisles, fire exits, emergency equipment or alarm pull stations with equipment or materials. Avoid the build-up of combustible trash and waste such as paper, wood and cardboard.

Flammable Safety Tips

Keep the use and storage of flammable and combustible materials to a minimum. Never use gasoline, benzine or similar flammable liquids indoors. Store flammable liquids in approved containers in well-ventilated storage areas. Never smoke near flammable liquids. Keep open flames away from walls, furniture, drapery and other flammable items. Keep matches and lighters out of children's reach. Repair leaking gas connections and store weed-killers, pesticides and other flammable products away from heat sources.
