UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

Background of the Issue

Fires are a serious safety issue in Myanmar because they can damage buildings, destroy property, injure people, and cause deaths. Fires may start in homes, shops, markets, schools, and workplaces. Electrical faults, unsafe cooking practices, and careless use of fire can increase the risk of fire outbreaks. When fires are not detected early, they can spread and become more difficult to control.

Fire prevention begins with awareness and preparation. By sharing reliable information and practical safety guidance, communities can become better prepared to reduce fire risks.

KNOW THE RISKS

Common Causes of Fires

Understanding common fire hazards is an important first step towards preventing fires and protecting people and property.

Electrical Hazards

Damaged wires, overloaded sockets, short circuits, and faulty appliances can create fire risks.

01 • ELECTRICAL

Cooking Accidents

Unattended cooking, unsafe stove use, and careless handling of cooking equipment can lead to fires.

02 • COOKING

Gas and Open Flames

Gas leaks, candles, mosquito coils, and open flames near flammable materials can cause dangerous situations.

03 • OPEN FLAMES

Hot and Dry Weather

Dry vegetation and hot conditions can increase the risk of outdoor fires spreading.

04 • WEATHER

Remember: Recognizing fire hazards early can help people take precautions and reduce risks.

Essential Safety Reminders

Important actions to remember during a fire.

Evacuate Safely

Leave the building using a safe exit and follow the designated evacuation route.

Contact Emergency Services

Call the appropriate local emergency services when you are in a safe location.

Warn Others if Safe

Alert nearby people while avoiding unnecessary exposure to danger.

Prepare Before an Emergency

Know your exits, identify safe meeting points, and understand your emergency plan.

Important: Prioritize personal safety. Never re-enter a burning building or delay evacuation to collect belongings.
