GOVERNMENT EMERGENCY SERVICE

Myanmar Fire Services Department

The Fire Services Department plays an important role in fire prevention, firefighting, rescue operations and public fire safety awareness.

The Myanmar Fire Services Department helps protect people and property by responding to fire emergencies, carrying out rescue operations and educating communities about fire safety.

Visit Government Website
02
HUMANITARIAN ORGANIZATION

Myanmar Red Cross Society

The Myanmar Red Cross Society provides humanitarian assistance, emergency relief, first aid and community support during disasters and other emergencies.

The Myanmar Red Cross Society supports vulnerable communities through first aid, emergency response, health services and disaster relief. It also helps communities prepare for different emergencies.

Visit Official Website
03
INTERNATIONAL FIRE SAFETY RESOURCES

National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)

The National Fire Protection Association provides fire safety information, educational resources and guidance to help prevent fires and protect communities.

NFPA is an international organization that develops fire and life safety standards. It provides educational materials and information about fire prevention, electrical safety and emergency preparedness.

Visit NFPA Website
