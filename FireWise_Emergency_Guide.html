EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

What Should You Do During a Fire?

Remember these essential steps if a fire occurs.

01

Recognize the Danger

If you notice a fire, smoke, or a fire alarm, alert people nearby immediately.

02

Evacuate Immediately

Leave the building using the nearest safe exit. Do not waste time collecting personal belongings.

03

Stay Low Under Smoke

If smoke is present, stay low while moving towards a safe exit. Smoke can make breathing difficult.

04

Use the Stairs

Avoid elevators during a fire. Use the stairs and follow the building's evacuation signs.

05

Reach a Safe Meeting Point

Move away from the building and gather at your designated assembly point.

06

Contact Emergency Services

Contact the local fire and emergency services. Give them the location and explain the situation.

IMPORTANT REMINDER

Never Re-enter a Burning Building

Once you have evacuated, stay outside in a safe place. Never return inside until emergency responders confirm that it is safe.
